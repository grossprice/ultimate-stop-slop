The same word ends in -ize in every form: organize, organized, organizing, organization.

## Em dashes

No em dashes (`—`) anywhere, and no spaced double hyphen (` -- `) standing in for one. Use a comma,
a colon, parentheses or two sentences.

The one exception is typography, not prose: a lone `—` in an empty table cell to mean "no
value".

## Case

**Sentence case** for headlines, headings, titles, buttons and labels: capitalize the first
word and proper nouns only.

| Avoid | Use instead |
|-------|-------------|
| "Pricing For Small Teams" | "Pricing for small teams" |
| "Start Your Free Trial" | "Start your free trial" |

**House option: headlines end with a full stop.** A project can turn this on. Then a headline
reads "Two plans." or "Measured daily. Is it working?", with a question mark where the headline
asks. SEO titles and meta titles never take the full stop.

## Punctuation

- **No exclamation points.** Excitement comes from the fact.
- **Percentages use %.** Never "pts" or "percentage points" in interface copy: "+4%".
